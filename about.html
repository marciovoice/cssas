About CSSAS

Chandra Shekhar Singh Ayurvedic Sansthan is a premier institution for Ayurvedic education in Varanasi, dedicated to preserving the ancient wisdom of Ayurveda while integrating modern scientific approaches.

Established with the vision of producing skilled vaidyas, the institute offers comprehensive programs in BAMS, MD/MS, and certificate courses, backed by clinical training in a 150‑bed hospital and a herbal garden with 200+ medicinal plants.

Our Mission

To provide authentic, evidence‑based Ayurvedic education that upholds the principles of classical texts, fosters research, and serves the community through compassionate healthcare.

Our Vision

To be a global leader in Ayurvedic education and healthcare, integrating traditional knowledge with modern technology to create a healthier world.

Leadership & Management

The institute is guided by a team of experienced academicians, visionary administrators, and renowned vaidyas who ensure the highest standards of education and patient care. The management is committed to continuous improvement and transparency.
