Rooted in tradition, built for the future

At Chandra Shekhar Singh Ayurvedic Sansthan, we believe that the wisdom of the Vedas holds the key to holistic wellness. Our mission is to train the next generation of Ayurvedic physicians who will carry this ancient knowledge forward with integrity and skill.

Located in the spiritual heart of Varanasi, our campus is a living laboratory of Ayurveda — from our herbal garden with over 200 medicinal plants to our 150-bed attached hospital where students gain real clinical experience.

  • Authentic curriculum based on Charaka Samhita & Sushruta Samhita
  • Faculty of renowned vaidyas and academicians
  • 150-bed Ayurvedic hospital for hands-on training
  • Herbal garden with 200+ medicinal plant species
